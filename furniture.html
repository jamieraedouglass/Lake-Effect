Custom furniture and millwork designed as an extension of the architectural vision—ensuring every room is complete, every detail intentional, and every piece made to last.

"Furniture is architecture at the human scale. The same principles apply: proportion, material, and the relationship between parts."

Our philosophy

Our furniture design practice grew organically from our architecture work. When clients asked for pieces that completed a room—a dining table scaled precisely to a dining room, a desk built into a library's alcove—we began designing and producing them.

Today, furniture design is a full offering. We work with trusted fabricators in the Chicago area to produce custom pieces in wood, metal, and upholstery—each built to specification and designed to last generations.

How we work

From sketch
to heirloom

01
Brief

We begin by understanding the room, the use, and the aesthetic direction. Dimensions, materials, and functional requirements are documented before any drawing begins.

02
Design

Measured drawings are produced to exact specification—often in the context of architectural drawings so the piece can be understood in its room.

03
Fabrication

We work with trusted Chicago-area craftspeople who share our commitment to quality. We oversee production from material selection through final finish.

04
Installation

Every piece is installed and adjusted on-site. We don't consider a commission complete until the piece is in place and exactly right.

Materials

What we work with

Walnut

American black walnut for tables, case goods, and millwork. Chosen for its richness and the way it ages.

White Oak

Versatile and beautiful, white oak takes stain beautifully and holds up to daily use. Our most-specified material.

Steel & Brass

Blackened steel bases and aged brass hardware add structural interest and longevity to wood-dominant pieces.

Natural Stone

Marble, limestone, and soapstone tops for kitchen and bath. We source slabs with our clients to ensure the right match.
