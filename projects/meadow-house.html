The project

Situated adjacent to a meadow and nestled among mature trees, the residence is carefully positioned to capture expansive views of the nature preserve maintained by the Lake Forest Open Lands Association.

The building's massing is composed of three distinct volumes, maximizing natural light and creating a series of protected terraces that strengthen the connection between the residence and its wooded setting.

The approximately 5,400-square-foot home includes four bedrooms and three-and-one-half bathrooms.
