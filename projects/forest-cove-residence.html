The project

Nestled on a beautifully wooded site in East Lake Bluff, this six-bedroom home showcases the timeless design principles of the turn-of-the-century Arts & Crafts movement.

The architecture intentionally pairs an abundance of natural light with a deep sense of tradition and history.

Beyond creating visually striking interior spaces, the home's unique layout extends outward to form dynamic outdoor rooms complete with elegant bluestone terraces and lush gardens.
