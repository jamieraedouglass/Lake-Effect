The project

Located in the Catskill Mountains, approximately two and one-half hours from New York City, this weekend retreat was designed to capture expansive views of Hunter Mountain. Constructed over an existing structure, the residence was conceived as a “house within a house,” allowing the new building envelope to incorporate and transform the original dwelling.

The vaulted living space and its south-facing wall of glass contribute to the home's passive environmental strategy. During the winter months, the space acts as a solar collector, while a heat pump distributes conditioned air throughout the house. In summer, a two-foot roof overhang shades the glazing, limiting direct solar gain while preserving views of the surrounding mountain landscape.
