The project

Built on the foundation of a 1950s ranch, this new house in the English Cottage style tradition is finished in stucco throughout, and warmly addresses the southern entry point into east Lake Bluff.

The house has a ground floor primary bedroom, a gourmet kitchen and a family room, with three bedrooms on the second floor.
