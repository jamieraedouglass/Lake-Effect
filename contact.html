Common Questions

Our usual patch is the North Shore, Lake County down through Evanston. We do take work further afield, but it's decided case by case. Tell us where you are and we'll give you a straight answer.

Full-service commissions are usually a percentage of construction cost. Consulting and partial services are hourly. Either way you'll have the fee structure in writing before you commit to anything.

Fill in the form on this page and we'll set up a short introductory call. We'll ask about the project, you can ask about how we work, and between us we'll work out whether it's a good fit.

For a new house, figure 12 to 18 months from the first meeting to permit, with construction adding another 12 to 24 on top. Renovations move a good deal faster. We'll give you a realistic timeline at the first meeting rather than an optimistic one.
